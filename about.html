About Me

Professional Profile

Home / About

I am a dedicated academician and researcher currently serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Government Engineering College, Khagaria, Bihar, India. With over 5 years of industry and academic experience, I am passionate about teaching, research, and mentoring the next generation of engineers.

I completed my M.Tech. in Control and Instrumentation from Dr. B. R. Ambedkar NIT Jalandhar, Punjab, India. I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. (Part-Time) in the Department of Instrumentation and Control Engineering at the NIT Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu, India.

My research interests include Control Theory and Applications, Model Order Reduction, Optimization Algorithms, Robotics, Renewable Energy Systems, and Control of Lower Extremity Exoskeletons.

To date, I have published 35+ research articles in reputed journals and conferences. I also serve as an Editor for the Journal of Control Science and Engineering (CSE) published by the Science Publishing Group.
